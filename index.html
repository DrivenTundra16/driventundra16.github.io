About

I am a third-year Applied Informatics student at Tula State University, specializing in intelligent and domain-oriented automated systems. My daily driver is NixOS: the entire machine — desktop, services, secrets — lives in a single flake, so a clean install is one command away.

Most of what I build sits between infrastructure and tooling: local-first AI gateways, Wayland desktop shells, Go services and Flutter apps. I like systems that can be rebuilt, explained and handed over — not ones that only work on the machine they were born on.
